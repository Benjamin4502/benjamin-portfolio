Prologue

Where it all started

I hold a Bachelor of Theology (Second Class Upper, Ezekiel College of Theology, 2025) and am completing a Diaconate Postgraduate Diploma at Lagos Anglican Seminary. As Vicar of Our Saviour's Anglican Church in Odo-Ragushin, I write and deliver sermons, draft liturgical documents, and handle diocesan correspondence every week — work that demands clarity, structure, and theological precision.

That same precision carries into my other work: building Shopify storefronts as a freelance developer, producing financial literacy content under Excel WealthPro, and evaluating AI-generated writing for accuracy and tone. Different domains, same standard for what counts as well-made.
